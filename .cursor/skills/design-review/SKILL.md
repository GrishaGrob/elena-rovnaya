# Premium design review

Use after each major design pass.

Review the page as a senior art director:
- Is the first screen recognizable as Elena's brand within 3 seconds?
- Does the layout feel editorial rather than template-driven?
- Are spacing, type scale and alignment intentional?
- Are Burgundy/Gold used as accents rather than decoration everywhere?
- Is Elena's portrait cropped naturally at every breakpoint?
- Are icons visually consistent and from the brand kit?
- Is there any AI-slop: blobs, glow, generic gradients, random bento, excessive pills?
- Does each section have a distinct compositional idea while belonging to one system?
- Is mobile genuinely designed, not compressed desktop?
- Does all Russian copy fit without awkward orphan lines or overflow?

Then fix issues rather than only listing them.
