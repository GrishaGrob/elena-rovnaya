# Website brief — Elena Rovanya

## Positioning
Elena Rovanya is presented as a psychologist working in a gestalt approach. The site should help a person recognize their situation, feel safe enough to make first contact, and understand Elena's way of working without overpromising outcomes.

## Core emotional promise
Not "I will fix you", but: you do not have to keep pretending that everything is fine; together we can look at what is happening now and search for a more stable inner point of support.

## Brand character
Warm, alive, feminine, intelligent, grounded, with inner force. Not passive calm. Not aggressive hustle. A sense of contained energy: "I can look at reality and move."

## Primary themes
1. Money — fear, shame, control, value, security, patterns of spending/earning and what money means emotionally.
2. Self-worth — value without constant proof.
3. Relationships — boundaries, repeating scenarios, breakup/separation and responsibility.
4. Inner support — periods when familiar stability disappeared.
5. Motherhood — remaining oneself inside family roles and social pressure.
6. Personal path — imperfection, rebuilding, movement after difficult periods.

## UX principles
- Recognition before explanation.
- Human language before professional terminology.
- Trust before conversion.
- One clear CTA per major section.
- The site may feel premium, but never distant or arrogant.
- Price/contact blocks must be data-driven and hidden if unconfirmed.

## Recommended section order
Header → Hero → Recognition themes → Gestalt explained → Money focus → About Elena → Motherhood/personal path → How consultation works → Reviews (only real) → FAQ → Final CTA → Footer.
